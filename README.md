## About Me 👋🏽

Hi, thanks for stopping by! I'm a data scientist working at the intersection of **machine learning and human health**. My research at the **Broad Institute of MIT &amp; Harvard** and **Massachusetts General Hospital** spans **predictive modeling, biomedical NLP, and explainable AI** applied to large-scale clinical and multi-omic data — building models *and* production-grade pipelines that turn messy, high-dimensional health data into reliable, interpretable insight.

I care about ML that holds up in the real world: **rigorous, reproducible, equitable across diverse populations, and explainable enough for domain experts to trust.** That mindset runs through my published work — from integrative proteomic models for cardiac disease, to open-source toolkits used by the wider research community, to methods that extend risk prediction fairly across ancestrally diverse patients.

🔭 **Currently focused on:** applied healthcare ML, clinical text mining, model interpretability, and scalable data pipelines.
